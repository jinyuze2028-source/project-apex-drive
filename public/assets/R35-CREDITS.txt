Project Apex Drive adapts the vehicle to its own physics and materials, strips the
source wheels and several unused interior/mechanical components, and adds rally wheels,
lighting, a fictional livery, roll cage and functioning instruments and controls.
The Nissan and GT-R names and marks identify the vehicle; no endorsement is implied.

Reference specifications and photography: Nissan GT-R press kit.
https://usa.nissannews.com/en-US/releases/2024-nissan-gt-r-press-kit
Tarmac rally reference: STILLEN's 2009 R35 GT-R build.
https://blog.stillen.com/tag/nissan/page/11/
The fictional game package is an original adaptation with approximate calibration.
The 18-inch APEX Tarmac R tread, sidewall, wheels, brakes and replacement
surface textures are original to Project Apex Drive. Tarmac tyre design reference:
https://www.michelinmotorsport.com/en/motorsport/tyres/michelin-pilot-sport-r-version-gt
The V6 audio is original procedural synthesis with no imported audio samples.
